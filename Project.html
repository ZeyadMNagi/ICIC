The Project



Our Sign Language-to-Speech Translation Glove
Our team is dedicated to addressing the communication gap faced by the deaf and hard-of-hearing community. The project, as depicted in the image, is the development of an innovative sign language-to-speech translation glove. This wearable device aims to convert sign language gestures into audible speech in real-time, facilitating seamless conversation with hearing individuals.

How It Works
The system consists of a pair of gloves equipped with advanced flex sensors on each finger. These sensors are designed to detect the subtle movements, bends, and spatial orientation of the hands and fingers as a user performs signs. This motion data is then transmitted to a central processing unit—a module integrated into a wearable garment like the hoodie shown. A custom-built machine learning algorithm processes this data, identifying and translating specific gestures into corresponding letters, words, or phrases. The final output is then converted into clear, natural-sounding audio through a speaker, allowing the user's message to be heard and understood by anyone.

Our Goal
This project goes beyond just a technological solution. We are committed to empowering the deaf and hard-of-hearing community by providing them with a tool that enhances their independence and social inclusion. By creating a device that is both accurate and comfortable to wear, we aim to reduce the feelings of embarrassment or frustration that can arise from communication barriers. Our glove is a step toward a more accessible world where every individual's voice can be heard.

Stage 1: Idea


When people think about helping others, they often get confused about how to do so. This is exactly what happened to us, but instead of giving up, we started learning and searching for answers.
We were shocked to learn that 10% of deaf and mute people are in Egypt, and the ways to help them are very limited.

That's when we decided to take action and make communication with them easier. After considering many ideas, we finally came up with the concept of creating The Sign Suit.
